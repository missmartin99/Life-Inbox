How Life Inbox uses your Google account

Sign in with Google

When you sign in, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with Life Inbox. They are used only to create your account, show who is signed in, and keep your inbox private to you.

Google Calendar (optional)

Google Calendar is off until you connect it in Settings → Connected apps. Life Inbox then asks for one permission: to create its own calendar and manage the events on it (calendar.app.created).

  • It makes a calendar called “Life Inbox” and adds your tasks that have a due date as events.
  • When a task changes, its event is updated. When you finish or dismiss a task, its event is removed.
  • It cannot see or change any of your other calendars or events. The permission doesn't allow it.
  • You can disconnect at any time. The “Life Inbox” calendar is deleted and the access is handed back to Google.

Life Inbox doesn't sell your data, use it for advertising, or use your Google data to train AI models. The privacy policy explains exactly what is stored and who it is shared with.
