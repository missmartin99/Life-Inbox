Privacy policy

Effective 13 November 2026

Life Inbox (“we”) is an app that collects your notes, links, files and voice memos, organizes them, and turns them into tasks. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Questions: CONTACT_EMAIL.

1. What we collect

From your Google account, when you sign in

  • Your name, email address and profile picture.
  • Your Google account ID, so we recognize you next time.

What you add to Life Inbox

  • Notes, links, PDFs, images and voice recordings, and the text taken from them.
  • The tasks, topics and labels made from them, and any changes you make.

Profile details you choose to add

  • A display name, an optional date of birth, and an optional photo. Uploaded photos are resized, and camera and location details are removed.
  • Your settings, such as theme and notification choices.

Sign-in sessions

  • A random sign-in token in a cookie. We store only a scrambled (hashed) copy of it, along with the type of browser you used. Sessions end when you sign out, or after 14 days.

Google Calendar, only if you connect it

  • Access tokens from Google, stored encrypted.
  • The email address of the Google account you connected.
  • The ID of the “Life Inbox” calendar and of the events Life Inbox created on it.

2. How we use it

Only to run Life Inbox for you: to show and organize your notes, find tasks and due dates, list what's pending, answer questions about your notes, and keep your tasks on your calendar if you asked for that. We don't sell your data, show ads, or build advertising profiles.

3. Google user data

Life Inbox asks Google for these permissions:

  • openid, email, profile: to sign you in with your name, email address and picture.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.app.created (only if you connect Google Calendar): to create a calendar called “Life Inbox” and manage the events on it.

With Google Calendar connected, Life Inbox:

  • creates one calendar named “Life Inbox” in your Google Calendar;
  • adds an event for each open task that has a due date (the task's text, its priority, the words you used for the date, and a link to Life Inbox), updates it when the task changes, and removes it when you finish, dismiss or delete the task;
  • never reads, changes or deletes any of your other calendars or events. The permission it asks for doesn't allow that.

Data we receive from Google is used only to provide these features. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI or machine-learning models. It isn't shared with anyone except as needed to provide these features, to comply with the law, or with your permission.

Life Inbox's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Other services we use

  • Google: for sign-in, and for Google Calendar if you connect it.
  • OpenAI, only when it is turned on for the Life Inbox server you use (Settings → Connected apps shows whether it is): the text of your notes (including text taken from links, PDFs and images), voice recordings to be transcribed, and questions you ask together with the related parts of your notes are sent to OpenAI to organize your notes and answer your questions. Your Google account details and calendar are never sent to OpenAI. When it is off, notes are organized by Life Inbox's built-in rules and nothing is sent.
  • Websites you save: when you save a link, Life Inbox visits that page to read its title and text.
  • Fonts: the app loads its fonts from Google Fonts and Fontshare, which see your device's IP address when the fonts download.

5. How your data is protected

  • Your notes, tasks, files and settings are private to your account. Other people using Life Inbox can't see them.
  • Google access tokens are encrypted, and sign-in tokens are stored only in scrambled form.
  • Uploaded files and photos can only be opened by their owner.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and only keep what Life Inbox needs.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your account and what you add are kept until you delete them or ask us to delete your account.
  • Deleting a note deletes it and its file, along with tasks that came only from that note.
  • Disconnecting Google Calendar deletes the stored tokens and calendar details.

7. Your choices

  • Edit or delete your notes at any time.
  • Change your name, date of birth or photo on the Edit profile page, or remove your photo.
  • Disconnect Google Calendar in Settings → Connected apps. The “Life Inbox” calendar is deleted and the access is handed back to Google. You can also remove Life Inbox's access at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • To get a copy of your data or to delete your account and everything in it, email CONTACT_EMAIL.

8. Children

Life Inbox is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
